Florida State Roleplay — Ticket TOS

By opening a ticket, you agree to the following:

  • Use tickets only for reports, appeals, support, partnerships, or questions.
  • Be respectful to staff and other members.
  • No spam or duplicate tickets.
  • Be honest. False information or fake evidence may result in punishment.
  • Stay on topic and follow staff instructions.
  • Be patient while waiting for a response.

Violations May Result In:

  • Ticket closure
  • Warning
  • Role removal
  • Loss of ticket access
  • Blacklist
  • Temporary or permanent ban
Thank you for helping keep Florida State Roleplay professional and organized.
